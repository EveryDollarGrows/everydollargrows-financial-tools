Build a holiday plan before shopping

Subtract savings already set aside from the holiday budget, then divide by the number of weekly deposits remaining. Weekly deposits are rounded up to the nearest cent so rounding does not leave you short. Include gifts, food, travel and other costs you intend to cover. Enter whole weeks from 1 to 520; the tool does not infer a shopping deadline.

Worked example

A $500 holiday target minus $125 saved leaves $375. Over 10 weeks, save $37.50 each week.
