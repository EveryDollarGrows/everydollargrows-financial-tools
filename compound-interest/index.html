Compound Interest Calculator

Project savings with monthly contributions and a hypothetical annual return.

Your estimate—

Educational estimates only. Actual outcomes depend on individual circumstances; interest and return assumptions are not guaranteed.

Contributions and growth are different

The model compounds monthly at annual rate ÷ 12 and adds deposits at each month-end. Years are converted to the nearest whole month. A nominal rate is used, not APY. The result separates your deposits from hypothetical growth. It excludes taxes, fees, inflation and market losses; actual investments do not earn a smooth fixed return.

Worked example

Starting with $1,000, adding $100 per month for one year at 0% gives $2,200: all contributions and no interest.
