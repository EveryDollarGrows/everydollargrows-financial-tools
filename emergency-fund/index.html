Choose the expenses you need to protect

Multiply essential monthly expenses by your chosen coverage period, then subtract existing emergency savings. Include housing, utilities, food, transport, insurance and required debt payments. Choose your own coverage period based on your circumstances; this tool does not decide an appropriate fund size for you.

Worked example

Essential costs of $2,400 × 3 months give a $7,200 target. If $1,200 is saved, the remaining gap is $6,000.
