Estimate how long it takes to pay off a fixed-rate debt.

Your estimate—

Educational estimates only. Actual outcomes depend on individual circumstances; interest and return assumptions are not guaranteed.

How the payoff estimate works

The model charges balance × annual rate ÷ 12 before each monthly payment. The last payment is reduced to the amount owed. The rate and payment stay constant; there are no new purchases, fees or skipped payments. Balances are not rounded to cents each month. Actual lender schedules, daily interest and payment dates can differ.

Worked example

A $1,000 balance at 0% interest with $300 monthly payments takes 4 months; the last payment is $100.
