How this savings estimate works

Each month, the calculator applies annual interest ÷ 12, then adds your contribution at the end of the month. It reports the first whole month that reaches your target, up to 1,200 months. The annual rate is a nominal rate, not APY. A 0% rate is useful for a conservative contribution-only plan.

Worked example

With a $1,000 goal, $100 already saved, $150 saved per month and 0% interest, the target takes 6 months.
